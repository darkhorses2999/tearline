Moonshot · system design

Data Gathering & Analysis Engine

⚖︎ Audit verdict: FIX-FIRST · benchmarked vs 29 repos
🧱 One line: a single-machine, file-first research lakehouse + replay engine for one frugal operator. Free data in → snapshot it so it can never lie → replay it through one honest engine → let the live market grade it. No servers. No cluster. Wakes the expensive brain only at milestones.

The one rule everything rests on

🔒 No future leak — structural, not disciplinary. An as-of clock + as-of-stamped storage mean a decision at time T physically cannot read a row stamped after T. Backed by a test that injects a future bar and asserts the engine can't see it. This is the #1 silent killer of backtests — we block it by construction.

The honest audit — what's NOT real yet

World-class design instinct. Vaporware engine.

The repo today is one 46-line script + this prose. No DuckDB, no Parquet, no replay engine. The design warns against "a polished plan mistaken for the thing itself" — and right now it is that thing.

Two honesty holes we fix, not hide:

• No free source gives delisted history / point-in-time fundamentals → crypto-only for now (BTC/ETH survivorship ≈ moot). Equities are OUT until a real source exists.
• Over-built for today: CPCV, two-tier engine, wash-sale tax — machinery for strategies that don't exist yet.
