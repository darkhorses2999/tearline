The machine · 5 steps

From a free price feed to a verdict you can trust

Each step does one job — and borrows a proven idea from the public quant world instead of reinventing it. Tap why it matters to go deeper.

01

Gather

Pull free daily prices from Yahoo Finance and Binance.

Why it matters

One adapter per source, all behind a single shape. If one free feed dies, the loop fails over to another instead of breaking.

Borrows OpenBB · ccxt

02

Lock

Freeze each day's data so it can never be quietly changed later.

Why it matters

Exchanges silently restate old prices. If we re-pulled and overwrote, yesterday's "fact" could change under us and a backtest would cheat unnoticed. So every pull is saved once, stamped, and never edited.

Borrows zipline bundles

03

Clean

Throw out broken rows loudly — not silently.

Why it matters

Negative prices, gaps, impossible highs — caught the moment data arrives, and the run stops. Better a loud halt than a poisoned result you'd go on to trust.

Borrows Pandera checks

04

Replay

Walk through history one day at a time, blind to tomorrow, and trade.

Why it matters

The same code runs the backtest, the paper-trade, and one day the real account. Costs and taxes come out of every trade. This is where the one rule lives.

Borrows Lean · Nautilus

05

Grade

The live market scores it. Our own backtest gets no vote.

Only money moving forward, through a real unseen future, counts as a pass. Everything before this is a rehearsal.

The honest floor
The one rule · the whole trick

No peeking at the future

Every decision can use only the days that already happened.

Tomorrow isn't off-limits — it physically isn't there to read. Picture one week. The decision is made on Thursday, so Monday through Thursday are fair game. Friday and Saturday haven't happened yet.
