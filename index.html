The problem

"Confident, current, and wrong" is the default.

In 2026 a Big-Four firm pulled an AI-assisted report after only 5 of its 45 citations checked out. A generic chatbot will happily hand you a competitor analysis full of stale numbers and citations that don't exist. For a decision with money on it, that's worse than nothing.

What you get

A one-page teardown of any competitor.

  • Positioning & current pricing — pulled live, not from memory
  • Real traction numbers (funding, scale, growth) — each linked to its source
  • Where they beat you, and where you beat them
  • The 2–3 most realistic ways they could die (the inversion)
  • The one strategic gap a competitor could exploit
  • A numbered source for every claim · UNVERIFIED tags where the record is thin

Why it's different

We show the receipts.

Anyone can prompt a chatbot. The hard part — and the whole product — is verification: grounding each claim in a real, current source, separating self-reported marketing from independent data, and admitting what isn't known instead of inventing it. That's the difference between a brief you can present to your board and one you have to apologize for.
