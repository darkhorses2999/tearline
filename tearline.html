Beehiiv — Competitive Teardown

June 2026 · ~3-min read · every numeric claim mapped to a source · estimates & unverifiable items tagged.

1 · What it is + positioning

Beehiiv is a newsletter/creator platform founded in 2021 by ex–Morning Brew operators (CEO Tyler Denk) [1]. The pitch: the operating system to grow and monetize an audience [2]. Headline weapon: it takes 0% of paid-subscription revenue [3] — a direct shot at Substack's 10% cut [8].

2 · Real traction

Platform scale (beehiiv's own 2026 report — self-reported): 28B emails sent in 2025, 255M readers, 41%+ open rates [4]. Beehiiv's own business (Sacra estimates): ~$30M annualized revenue as of Jun 2025, up from ~$13M ARR Apr 2024 [5]. Funding: ~$50M total incl. a $33M Series B (Apr 2024, NEA) [6]. No 2025/26 round in public sources — UNVERIFIED.

3 · Landscape

4 · How Beehiiv dies (inversion)

  • Deliverability collapse on shared sending infrastructure — abuse by some senders dragged inbox placement for all; spam complaints surfaced 2024 [7]. The product's whole value is the email arriving. (Current severity UNVERIFIED.)
  • The 0% moat is a price — a better-funded rival can match it.
  • Single-channel platform risk — AI-summarized inboxes / sender-rule shifts compress every newsletter platform at once.

5 · The one strategic gap

Beehiiv monetizes publishers brilliantly but has no reader-side discovery network like Substack's. Pair beehiiv-grade economics (0% cut) with a real reader-facing discovery surface and you hit it where it's thinnest. The wedge: "Substack's network, beehiiv's economics."
