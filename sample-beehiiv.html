Beehiiv — Competitive Teardown

June 2026 · ~3-min read · every numeric claim mapped to a numbered source below · estimates and unverifiable items tagged.

1 · What it is + positioning

Beehiiv is a newsletter/creator platform founded in 2021 by ex–Morning Brew operators (CEO Tyler Denk) [1]. The pitch isn't "a place to write" but the operating system to grow and monetize an audience — editor + site + email + a built-in growth/ad machine [2]. Headline weapon: it takes 0% of paid-subscription revenue (you pay only Stripe's fee) [4] — a direct shot at Substack's 10% cut [10].

2 · Real traction

Platform scale (beehiiv's own State of Newsletters 2026 — self-reported): 28B emails sent in 2025, 255M unique readers, 41%+ open rates [5]. Creator paid-subscription revenue hit $19M in 2025, ~+138% YoY [5].

Beehiiv's own business (Sacra estimates): ~$30M annualized revenue as of Jun 2025, up from ~$13M ARR in Apr 2024 [6]. Funding: ~$50M total, incl. a $33M Series B (Apr 2024, led by NEA) [7]. No 2025/2026 round in public sources — UNVERIFIED.

Wins: economics (0% cut) and the growth toolkit — best for running a newsletter as a business. Loses: Substack owns discovery + cultural gravity; Kit wins on automation for sellers; Ghost wins on ownership.

4 · How Beehiiv dies (the inversion)

  • Deliverability collapse on shared sending infrastructure — abuse by some senders dragged inbox placement for all; spam complaints surfaced in 2024 [9]. A newsletter platform's whole value is the email arriving. (Current severity UNVERIFIED; structural risk real.)
  • The 0% moat is a price, and prices get matched. The wedge is economic, not technical — a better-funded rival can neutralize it.
  • Single-channel platform risk. AI-summarized inboxes / tighter sender rules compress every newsletter platform at once — and beehiiv is the most email-dependent of the majors.

5 · The one strategic gap

Beehiiv monetizes publishers brilliantly but has no reader-side discovery network like Substack's app/Notes flywheel. A competitor pairing beehiiv-grade economics (0% cut) with a real reader-facing discovery surface would hit beehiiv exactly where it's thinnest — while Substack's 10% cut leaves its own economic flank open. The wedge: "Substack's network, beehiiv's economics."
